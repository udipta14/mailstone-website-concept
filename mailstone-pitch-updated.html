When should results begin?

First enquiries may arrive within 1–2 weeks of live ads. Review early quality after 4–6 live weeks. Organic search visibility needs months. A delayed launch means unused media is held and reconciled.

What about social growth?

Growth planning goals after a 30-day baseline: 50–150 net new followers and 40–120 content interactions per month across LinkedIn and Instagram. Relevant profiles and enquiries matter most. These are provisional goals, not guarantees.

Lead buying and influencer marketing

Optional tests: INR 10,000–INR 15,000 for a verified commercial lead supplier, or INR 20,000–INR 40,000 for a relevant specialist collaboration. Get vendor quotes and track lead quality. Both sit outside the package.

What will the monthly report show?

Spend by category and platform, relevant traffic, enquiries, project-fit details, duplicate or spam rate, and media-only versus total marketing cost per lead. Sales reports meetings, proposals and conversions separately.
